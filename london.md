# London

London, the capital of the United Kingdom, is a city known for its rich history, iconic landmarks, and vibrant cultural scene.

## Buckingham Palace

Buckingham Palace is the official residence of the British monarch. It is an iconic symbol of the British monarchy and a must-visit landmark in London.
> TIP: Visit during the Changing of the Guard ceremony for a unique experience.
![open1](./buckingham_palace.jpeg)
![openFra](./buckingham_palace2.jpeg)

## Tower of London

The Tower of London is a historic castle located on the north bank of the River Thames. It is well known for housing the Crown Jewels and its fascinating history.
> Don't miss the guided tours by the Yeoman Warders, also known as Beefeaters.
![open1](./tower_of_london.jpeg)
![openFra](./tower_of_london2.jpeg)

## The British Museum

The British Museum is one of the world's largest and most comprehensive museums, dedicated to human history, art, and culture. It houses an extensive collection of artifacts from around the world.
> TIP: Entry to the museum is free, but special exhibitions may require tickets.
![open1](./british_museum.jpeg)
![openFra](./british_museum2.jpeg)
