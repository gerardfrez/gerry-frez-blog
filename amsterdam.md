# Amsterdam

Amsterdam, the capital of the Netherlands, is known for its artistic heritage, elaborate canal system, and narrow houses with gabled facades.

## Rijksmuseum

The Rijksmuseum is a Dutch national museum dedicated to arts and history in Amsterdam. It is renowned for its collection of masterpieces by Dutch artists such as Rembrandt and Vermeer.
> TIP: Visit early in the morning to avoid the crowds.

![openFra](./rijksmuseum2.jpeg)

## Anne Frank House

The Anne Frank House is a museum dedicated to Jewish wartime diarist Anne Frank. The museum preserves the hiding place where Anne Frank wrote her diary during World War II.
> Don't miss the sobering experience of the Secret Annex.
![open1](./anne_frank_house.jpeg)
![openFra](./anne_frank_house2.jpeg)

## Van Gogh Museum

The Van Gogh Museum is dedicated to the works of Vincent van Gogh and his contemporaries. It houses the largest collection of Van Gogh's paintings and drawings in the world.
> TIP: Buy tickets online in advance to skip the line.
![open1](./van_gogh_museum.jpeg)
![openFra](./van_gogh_museum2.jpeg)
