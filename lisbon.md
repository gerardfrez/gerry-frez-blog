# Lisbon

Lisbon, the capital of Portugal, is a city known for its hilly, coastal landscape, historic architecture, and vibrant culture.

## Belem Tower

The Belem Tower is a fortified tower located in the civil parish of Santa Maria de Belém. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site and a symbol of Portugal's Age of Discovery.
> TIP: Visit in the morning to avoid the crowds.
![open1](./belem_tower.jpeg)
![openFra](./belem_tower2.jpeg)

## Jeronimos Monastery

The Jeronimos Monastery is a former monastery of the Order of Saint Jerome near the Tagus river in the parish of Belém. It is a stunning example of Manueline architecture.
> Don't miss the beautiful cloisters and the tomb of Vasco da Gama.
![open1](./jeronimos.jpeg)
![openFra](./jeronimos2.jpeg)

## Alfama District

The Alfama District is the oldest district of Lisbon, known for its narrow streets, traditional Fado music, and historic buildings. It offers a glimpse into the city's Moorish past.
> TIP: Take a tram ride through the district for a unique experience.
![open1](./alfama.jpeg)
![openFra](./alfama2.jpeg)

## Portuguese Cuisine

> TIP: Must-try dishes include pastel de nata (custard tarts), bacalhau (salt cod), and a variety of seafood dishes. Don't forget to sample the local wines and port.
